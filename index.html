What I'm doing lately

  • Power electronics for deployable systems

    I developed power electronics for deployable hardware, including control and power-selection boards, buck-boost supplies and a capacitor-based UPS. The work involved making several subsystems behave reliably together: power conversion, switching, embedded control and the equipment being powered.

    My reported results included 97% converter efficiency and power-source switching below 2 ms. The systems supported deployments where reducing manual intervention and maintaining power continuity were important.

  • Automated wire-harness validation

    I built a modular test rig to automate wire-harness validation. The system checked for faults automatically and reduced validation time by up to 90% compared with the previous process.

    This project brought together electronics, test logic and practical workflow design. The objective was to make a recurring engineering task faster and more repeatable while giving the operator useful fault information.

  • Assistive-exoskeleton controls and HIL

    As Control Systems Lead for an assistive-exoskeleton thesis project, I developed state-machine logic and a hardware-in-the-loop test setup for actuator control.

    The ESP32-S3-based rig enabled testing of PID control at 1 kHz across four CubeMars actuators. I also led coordination within a five-person team integrating 12 sensors and the associated software systems.

    The project connected control modelling, embedded implementation and physical testing, with particular attention to how the system behaved during integration.

  • Battery BLE reverse-engineering and telemetry

    I reverse-engineered an undocumented BLE interface to make battery information accessible within a wider deployed system. I developed a React Native application and a Raspberry Pi telemetry deployment around the recovered protocol.

    The project required working across the device interface, application software and deployment environment. The integrated solution was delivered to the Royal Australian Air Force.

  • Sunswift vehicle communications

    At Sunswift Racing, I worked on embedded communications and telemetry for a solar vehicle. I architected a CAN FD network carrying data from 12 nodes and developed Dash Connect, a 5G V2X module supporting driver awareness.

    I also integrated AWS IoT Core and Grafana into a live telemetry system. Working within a large multidisciplinary engineering team required careful coordination across interfaces, testing and deployment.

  • Digital accessibility at TPG Telecom

    At TPG Telecom, I assessed digital accessibility across 15 product areas and presented findings to senior leadership. I worked across design and engineering teams to turn the findings into five prioritised remediation plans.

    This experience strengthened my ability to explain technical problems to different audiences, structure recommendations and work across organisational boundaries.
